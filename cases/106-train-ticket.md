# 106. Train ticket

The Train ticket page sells one train ticket, Warsaw to Kraków, for 49.00 PLN, in three steps:
Journey, Extras and Pay.

**Requirements to test:**

- The order the user gets without changing anything is the ticket alone, for 49.00 PLN, with no
  offers by email.
- A seat (+5.00 PLN) and travel insurance (+9.00 PLN) are added only when the user ticks them.
  Each box's label says what ticking it adds.
- The total shown on every step is what the user pays. Nothing is added at the end.
- Going Back from Pay to Extras keeps what the user chose.
