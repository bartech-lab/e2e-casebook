# 202. Ferry tickets

The Ferry tickets page prices one crossing from Hel to Gdynia for a number of adults and children under 12. There is no server.

**Requirements to test:**

- An adult ticket costs 32.00 PLN. A child under 12 pays half: 16.00 PLN.
- Two adults with two or more children get a family discount of 10% of the tickets, rounded to the grosz, shown as its own line.
- The total is the tickets minus the discount.
- A booking holds at most 9 passengers; with more, the page says "At most 9 passengers per booking." instead of a price.
