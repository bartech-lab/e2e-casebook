# 109. Floor plan

The Floor plan page shows the plan of a flat. The zoom is shown as a percentage. What it
promises:

- The zoom goes from 100% to 400% and never past either end. **Zoom in** and **Zoom out** change
  it by 50 points and are disabled at the ends; **Reset** returns to 100%.
- On a touch screen, pinching the plan with two fingers zooms the plan, not the page. Moving the
  fingers apart zooms in, together zooms out, in proportion to the distance between them.
- With a mouse or trackpad, Ctrl and the wheel over the plan zoom the plan (wheel up or a pinch
  outwards zooms in) and never the page.
- The wheel without Ctrl scrolls the page as usual, also over the plan.
