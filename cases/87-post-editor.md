# 87. Post editor

The Post editor page has a list of your posts on the left and a form for a new post in a panel
on the right. The form is taller than the panel.

**Requirements to test:**

- Everything in the form can be reached with the mouse wheel, the keyboard (Tab), and a
  touchpad, down to **Publish**.
- **Add question** adds a question and its answer, and puts the cursor in the new question.
- **Publish** saves the title, the body, every question that has text, and **Pin to the top of
  the blog**. The post appears under Your posts, marked "(pinned)" when pinned, and the form
  empties.
