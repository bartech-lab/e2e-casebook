# 77. Appearance

The Appearance page lets you pick a theme and how much motion you see.

**Requirements to test:**

- "Same as my device" follows the device's light or dark setting and its reduce-motion setting,
  and changes as soon as the device setting changes.
- Your choice is saved to your account, so it follows you to another device. On the device you
  chose it on, the page opens in your theme from the first frame.
- When another device saved a newer choice, the page switches to it once it loads.
- Text in the preview stays readable in both themes, at a contrast of at least 4.5:1.
- With motion reduced, nothing in the preview animates.
