# 76. Country

The Country page is a list of 30 countries you can pick from with the keyboard.

**Requirements to test:**

- Typing letters in quick succession jumps to the first country that starts with them, with or
  without accents. After half a second without typing, the next letter starts a new search.
- Typing the same letter again moves on to the next country with that letter.
- Screen readers follow the highlighted country, the list scrolls to it, and Enter picks it.
