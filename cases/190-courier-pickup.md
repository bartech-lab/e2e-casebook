# 190. Courier pickup

The Courier pickup page is a form in two columns: Name and Phone, Street across both columns, Postcode and City, Pickup date, and Notes across both columns, then the buttons Back, Save draft and Book pickup in a row. **Book pickup** says "Pickup booked for <date>." when every field except Notes is filled in. There is no server.

**Requirements to test:**

- Tab moves through the fields and buttons in the order a person reads them: left to right along a row, then down to the next row.
- Every place Tab stops is on screen and visible.
- Tab stops on each field and button once, and nowhere else in the form.
