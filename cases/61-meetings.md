# 61. Meetings

The Meetings page adds a meeting at a date and time, once or every week for 4 weeks.

**Requirements to test:**

- You enter the time in your own time zone. A weekly meeting keeps that time on every week, also
  when the clocks change.
- Everyone sees every meeting in their own time zone, grouped by their own day and in time
  order, with the zone it was set in when that differs from theirs.
