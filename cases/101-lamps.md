# 101. Lamps

The Lamps page shows product cards. Each card has two round buttons, **Save** (♡) and
**Compare** (⇄).

**Requirements to test:**

- With a mouse, a card's buttons appear while the pointer is over the card and stay hidden
  otherwise, so the photos stay clean.
- They also appear while keyboard focus is on either of them.
- On a touch screen, where nothing can hover, the buttons are always shown.
- On a touch screen each button is at least 44 by 44 CSS pixels.
- Save and Compare are toggle buttons that say whether they are on. The line under the cards
  counts saved and compared lamps.
