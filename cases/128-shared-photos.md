# 128. Shared photos

The Shared photos page shows nine photos in a grid.
**Requirements to test:**

- Tapping or clicking a photo opens it; **Back to photos** returns to the grid.
- On a touch screen, pressing and holding a photo for half a second starts selecting and selects
  that photo. Lifting the finger afterwards does not open it. A shorter press is a tap.
- A finger that moves (to scroll the page) while pressing does not select anything.
- While selecting, tapping or clicking a photo selects or unselects it, says whether it is
  selected, and the bar shows "N photos selected" ("1 photo selected" for one). **Done** stops
  selecting and clears the selection.
- **Select** starts selecting without a long press, for mouse and keyboard users.
