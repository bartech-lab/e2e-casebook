# 68. Packing list

The Packing list page edits a list of things to pack.

**Requirements to test:**

- Every change can be undone and redone: with the Undo and Redo buttons, or with Ctrl+Z, and
  Ctrl+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y (Cmd on a Mac).
- Quick clicks on + or − for one item within a second count as one change.
- A new change after an undo ends the redo history.
- In a text field, the keys undo your typing, not the list.
