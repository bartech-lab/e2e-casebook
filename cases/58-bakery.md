# 58. Bakery

The Bakery page has a site header with four links.

**Requirements to test:**

- Below 640 px wide, the links fold into a **Menu** button; from 640 px up they sit in the header.
- The Menu button says whether the menu is open. While it is open, the page behind does not
  scroll. Escape closes it and puts focus back on the button.
- A closed menu cannot be reached with the keyboard or a screen reader.
- Picking a link shows its section and closes the menu. Resizing the window closes it too.
